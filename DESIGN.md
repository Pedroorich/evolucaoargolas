## Visual Concept: Minimalist Industrial Contrast
A bespoke, high-craft corporate aesthetic combining a luminous, minimal white Hero section with an immersion into deep dark industrial sections below. Built around the official color palette: White, Orange (`#F06602`), Royal Blue (`#0023CB`), and Black/Dark Slate (`#070A10`).

- **Brand Orange (`--color-orange`):** `#F06602` (Primary action, key highlights, active tags)

- **Royal Blue (`--color-blue`):** `#0023CB` (Technical credentials, secondary buttons, precision badges)
- **Pure White (`--color-white`):** `#FFFFFF` (Hero background, text in dark sections)
- **Deep Slate Ground (`--color-dark-bg`):** `#070A10` (Sections below Hero)

- **Light Gray Surface (`--color-light-bg`):** `#F8F9FA` (Hero secondary elements)
